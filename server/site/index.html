GovJob Radar

Central, State and Deputation government vacancies in India — in one place.

Not a government app. GovJob Radar is an independent app by DhruvaX Technologies. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing any government, ministry, department or recruiting body. All information is taken automatically from the official websites of the recruiting organisations and summarised with the help of AI. Every listing is checked against its official notice and links to it, but we cannot guarantee 100% correctness. Always check the official notification — it is the only authoritative source — and cross-check every detail before applying.

Where the information comes from

Every night the system checks the official websites of UPSC, SSC, Railways, ISRO, DRDO, the defence forces, central armed police forces, banks, ministries (for deputation circulars) and the State Public Service Commissions. Each vacancy shows its source and the date its official link was last verified.
