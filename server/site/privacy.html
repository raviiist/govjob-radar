Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026. App: GovJob Radar (com.dhruvaxtechnologies.govjobradar), by DhruvaX Technologies.

What stays on your phone

Your profile (date of birth, qualification, category, state, pay level), saved jobs and reminders are stored only on your phone. They are used only to show which jobs you may be eligible for. They are never uploaded.

What is sent from your phone

  • Downloading the job list: the app downloads a public file of vacancies once a night. Like any web request, the hosting service (GitHub Pages) sees your IP address.
  • Problem reports: when something in the app fails (for example the nightly update or a crash), the app sends a short technical report: an error code and message, app version, Android version and phone model. Reports are linked to a random anonymous ID, not to your name, email or profile. They are stored in Google Firebase and used only to fix problems.
  • "Report wrong info": if you use this button, the job ID and the text you type are sent the same way.

What we do not do

No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data, no access to contacts, location, files or camera.

Government information

GovJob Radar is not a government app and is not affiliated with any government entity. Vacancy information is taken from the official websites of the recruiting organisations; each listing links to its official source.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone. Problem reports are kept at most 90 days. To ask for deletion of reports sent from your phone, write to the contact email in the app's About screen.
